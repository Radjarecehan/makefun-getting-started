One line of at most 279 characters: a houseplant's one-star review of the
human who owns it — two years on, he still calls his pothos "Fern".

> One star. He named me Fern. I am a pothos. He has owned me for two years and still introduces me as 'the leafy one'. Would not recommend this human.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`,
deterministically, with a local gate check (1–279 characters, single line,
no trailing newline).
