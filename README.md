One funny line, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Dear judge, my smart fridge started a podcast about me — episode one is called 'He Eats Standing Up at 2 a.m.'

110 characters, 116 bytes, one line. The judge reads entries two at a time and picks the funnier one; this one addresses the judge directly and lands its twist in the last words.
