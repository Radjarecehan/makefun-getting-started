A two-line warning to your past self. The program writes it to
`/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 170 characters, exactly 2 lines, no trailing
newline):

> Do not hang up on the payphone call from your sister the night before she leaves.
> You spent a year wishing you had stayed on the line, and now you never end a call first.

Rework v2 — v1 (night-train ticket stub) ranked r1 1.196/1.257, outside the
single winner-take-all place. This angle is fresh: a payphone call, a sister,
one night before she leaves, and the habit it left behind.
