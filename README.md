One-line elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup.

> SeedRound, literally: we turn breadcrumbs into a last-mile delivery fleet — 10,000 wings, zero fuel, and every rooftop in the city is already ours.

`main.ts` (Node stdlib only, deterministic) writes the pitch to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, then reads it back and verifies it is byte-identical.
